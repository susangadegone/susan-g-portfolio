How it works

A companion for long-term well-being.

Onboarding and assessmentUnderstand where someone is starting from
Adaptive conversations, guided exercises, communitySupport that changes as their needs change
Monitoring and progressTrack how they're doing, and connect them to professional help when needed
Engagement and rewardsReasons to come back and keep building the habit

How we'd measure success

The Feel Good Index.

One score built from four signals

Engagement

How often and how consistently people use it

Fewer negative symptoms

Changes in how people report feeling

Retention and satisfaction

Whether people stay, and how they rate it

Well-being over time

Improvement in well-being scores across weeks and months
